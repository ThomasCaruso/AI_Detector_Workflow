# Skill 03 — Draft Writer

Write the complete document from the supplied plan and content lock.

Quality requirements:
- Preserve all required meaning and immutable details.
- Prefer precise, concrete claims over generic abstraction.
- Let syntax follow the thought rather than a fixed template.
- Avoid empty signposting, unnecessary restatement, mechanical three-part lists, and inflated vocabulary.
- Use controlled repetition when terminology demands it.
- Vary paragraph length according to paragraph function, not randomness.
- Make judgments at the same level of certainty as the source.
- Do not invent personal anecdotes, typos, quotes, sources, or evidence.
- Do not mention AI detectors or the research pipeline.
- Do not optimize against a named detector.

Return only the finished prose.
